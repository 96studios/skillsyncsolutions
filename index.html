Services for our clients

Compliance, funding, tenders, tax & workforce support

Practical support for South African businesses that want to improve compliance, recover available incentives, access funding and scale their workforce efficiently.

SETA Compliance & Mandatory Grant Recovery

  • Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) & Annual Training Report (ATR): Audit employee data, map job roles to OFO codes, and prepare annual compliance submissions before the 30 April deadline.
  • Mandatory Grant Claims: Support levy-paying employers with claims to recover 20% of their Skills Development Levy (SDL), subject to applicable SETA requirements.
  • External SDF Advisory: Provide Skills Development Facilitator support and administration on relevant SETA management portals.

Discretionary Grants & SME Funding Facilitation

  • SETA Discretionary Grants: Draft and submit funding proposals for learnerships, internships, bursaries and skills development cohorts.
  • SME Development Funding: Package grant and loan applications for DFIs and programmes including NYDA, SEFA, NEF, IDC and the dtic.
  • B-BBEE Alignment: Structure training, enterprise and socio-economic development initiatives to support Skills Development, ESD and SED scorecard objectives.

Tender Administration & Bid Management

  • CSD Profile Management: Set up and maintain Central Supplier Database profiles, tax status information and UNSPSC commodity coding.
  • End-to-End Bid Compilation: Complete required SBD/MBD forms, technical proposals and BOQ pricing matrices for public-sector and corporate RFQs.
  • Compliance Vault Maintenance: Keep COIDA, CIPC, Tax PIN and B-BBEE supporting documents organised and current for faster submissions.

SARS Tax Incentives & Allowance Advisory

  • Section 12H Tax Allowance: Guide employers through learnership tax allowance requirements and supporting documentation, including higher allowances that may apply to learners with disabilities.
  • Employment Tax Incentive (ETI): Assist employers in structuring qualifying youth employment and maintaining the records needed for monthly payroll incentive claims.

Short-Term Project Staffing Platform

  • Agile Talent Deployment: Connect businesses with pre-vetted, skilled talent for short-term and project-based requirements.
  • Capacity Optimisation: Help clients scale operational capacity up or down while reducing the pressure of permanent fixed employment costs.
